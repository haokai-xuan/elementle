How to play

Guess the daily element in 8 tries.

Type an element, pick a suggestion, then press Guess.

  • ⬆️ / ⬇️ The answer’s atomic number is higher / lower.
  • 🟩 Green letter: correct letter and position.
  • 🟨 Yellow letter: correct letter, wrong position.
  • ⬜ Uncoloured letter: not in the answer’s symbol.
  • 🟩 Green family: same element family.
  • 💡 Stuck? Tap Hint.
  • 🕛 New puzzle at midnight, your local time.
