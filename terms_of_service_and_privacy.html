Accounts, saved games and game ratings

You are responsible for keeping your sign-in credentials secure and for activity on your account. Choose a username that you are comfortable displaying publicly. Contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Registered users can revisit their own completed daily games from the streak calendar, including saved guesses, feedback, hint use and the elELO change recorded for that day, without a Lab subscription. Games played without signing in are not automatically added to an account's history. A game that has no recorded elELO result may still be available to view.

elELO ratings, game scores and elBot suggestions are automated game features. They depend on the saved guesses, hints, puzzle and scoring method. Ratings and scoring methods may change as the game develops. elBot analysis requires an active Lab entitlement; seeing an analysis button does not itself grant access. Archive and freeplay games are separate practice games and do not change your daily elELO rating or daily streak.

Elementle Lab subscriptions

Elementle Lab costs $1.99 USD per month or $17.99 USD per year. The annual plan is billed as one payment of $17.99 for the year, rather than monthly installments. Eligible accounts receive a seven-day free trial, available once per account, with a payment method required. Checkout displays your selected plan, trial and billing terms before you subscribe.

Unless you cancel, your saved payment method is charged when the trial ends and the subscription renews automatically each month or year according to your selected plan. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the first subscription charge, or before your next renewal to stop future renewal charges. Signing out or clearing browser storage does not cancel a subscription.

While signed in, use Manage billing or Manage subscription to open Stripe's billing portal and cancel. Use Update payment method on the Account page to change your payment method securely through Stripe. Your account and the billing portal display subscription status, plan, relevant billing dates and payment-method information. When a cancellation is scheduled for the end of a trial or paid period, access continues until the displayed access-end date. If a subscription ends immediately, access ends when the cancellation is confirmed. Cancellation stops future renewal; questions about charges or refund requests can be sent to help@elementlegame.com. Nothing in these terms limits any cancellation or refund rights required by applicable law.

Lab includes archives, freeplay, the vault, no advertising, elBot game analysis and capacity for two streak freezes instead of one. Increasing capacity does not grant an extra freeze; freezes are earned through daily solves. When Lab access ends, the stored freeze balance is reduced to a maximum of one when the account is next reconciled, such as during a daily game or stats update. Vault statistics count daily games from the local calendar date of your first Lab subscription, including its free trial, using the timezone recorded at checkout. Older subscriptions without recorded timezone information use UTC. That start date stays the same if you change timezone, cancel or later resubscribe; subsequent subscriptions do not reset your collection. Daily games on or after that date remain eligible even during a gap in membership, but viewing the vault requires active Lab access. Previously completed daily games and their elELO history remain available to the account; Lab features become unavailable until access is restored.

If a subscription payment fails, Lab stays available during a three-day grace period while Stripe schedules a retry. If the retry fails or payment is not recovered by the grace deadline, Lab access is suspended. Updating your payment method and settling the outstanding invoice through Stripe restores access once payment is confirmed. Elementle sends subscription-activation and cancellation emails, a trial reminder scheduled three days before the trial ends, and payment-failure or recovery notices to your account email. Keep your email and payment information current and use the billing dates shown in your account to manage renewal.

Stripe processes checkout and billing. We send Stripe your account email and an internal account identifier and store Stripe customer/subscription identifiers, subscription status and dates, your first subscription timestamp, fixed local start date and checkout timezone for vault statistics, and whether your account has used a trial. Full payment card numbers and security codes are entered on Stripe's hosted pages and are not stored by Elementle. We receive and store a limited payment-method summary, which may include the method type, card brand, last four digits and expiration month and year, to display billing information and explain payment failures. Stripe's handling of payment and checkout data is described in its Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

The following sections describe how Elementle handles account, game, billing and technical information.

Information we collect

You can play without creating an account. If you create one, we process the information you submit, such as your username, email address and account credentials, to create and secure the account and to sync game progress and statistics. For password accounts, passwords are stored as password hashes. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive your verified email address and a Google account identifier to authenticate and link your account; we do not receive your Google password. We also process saved guesses in order, puzzle dates and answers, results, hint use, streaks, freeze balances, elELO ratings and rating changes, calculated game scores, and archive or freeplay progress needed to provide those features.

Like most websites, our hosting and service providers may receive technical information sent by your browser when you visit, such as IP address, device and browser information, requested pages, date and time, and diagnostic or security logs. If you email us, we receive the information in that message and your email address.

Saved games, elBot and public leaderboards

Daily game history is associated with your account and is retrieved after sign-in. Archive and freeplay games are also stored when you use those features, so saved games can be revisited or analyzed. elBot processes a game's guesses, answer, hint use and scoring references to calculate feedback and suggestions. We use game results and scores to build community statistics and puzzle-difficulty references.

Public leaderboards can display your username, streak or elELO rating, rank or rating tier, and a small Lab icon when your account currently has Lab access. Other visitors can see that information. Your email address, payment details and individual guess history are not included in public leaderboard responses. You can also choose to share a game result yourself.

Browser storage

Elementle uses browser local storage and session storage to remember game progress, sign-up reminder preferences, cached statistics, analysis context and interface state on the device you use. For example, this can include submitted guesses, the number of guesses, whether a puzzle is complete and, after sign-in, a session token. Local storage remains on your device until it is cleared by you, your browser or the site. Clearing it can reset local game progress or sign you out, but it does not delete server-side account history or cancel a Stripe subscription.

A secure, HttpOnly cookie lets the server check your membership and disable subscriber ads before sending a page. It lasts up to 30 days and is cleared on logout.

Elementle uses Mediavine for advertising. Its script loads on every page. Ads are disabled for Lab members and signed-in visitors whose membership cannot be verified.

Elementle uses Google Analytics to understand use of the site. Google may process browser and device information, identifiers, IP addresses and site activity as described in its Partner Sites policy and Privacy Policy.

Where available, use the Manage Preferences link provided by Mediavine in the footer of an advertising-enabled page to review or change advertising consent. Advertising controls are available on pages where the advertising script has loaded. Browser cookie controls also let you block or delete cookies. Advertising consent choices do not necessarily control separate services such as Google Analytics.

How we use information

When you finish the main game without an account, we use a random browser identifier to avoid counting the same puzzle result twice. We store a hashed version of that identifier with the puzzle date, guesses, result, hint usage and calculated game scores to build community statistics and difficulty references. Results waiting to upload are saved in your browser and retried when you return or reconnect.

We use account and game information to provide the game, authenticate users, save progress, show statistics, respond to support requests, protect the service and investigate misuse. We use technical information to keep the site reliable and secure. We use analytics and advertising information as described above and only to the extent allowed by your settings and applicable law.
