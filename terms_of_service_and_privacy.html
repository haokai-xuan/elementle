Information we collect

You can play without creating an account. If you create one, we process the information you submit, such as your username, email address and account credentials, to create and secure the account and to sync game progress and statistics. We also process the guesses, results and account statistics needed to provide those features.

Like most websites, our hosting and service providers may receive technical information sent by your browser when you visit, such as IP address, device and browser information, requested pages, date and time, and diagnostic or security logs. If you email us, we receive the information in that message and your email address.

Browser storage

Elementle uses browser local storage to remember game progress and interface state on the device you use. For example, this can include submitted guesses, the number of guesses, whether a puzzle is complete and, after sign-in, a session token. Local storage remains on your device until it is cleared by you, your browser or the site. Clearing it can reset local game progress or sign you out.

Elementle uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate use of the site and Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, identifiers, IP address, browser information, activity on this site and other information described in their policies to measure advertising, limit fraud, report performance and, where permitted, personalise ads.

Google explains how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services in its Partner Sites policy and provides controls in My Ad Center. Google also publishes its Privacy Policy. These external services have their own terms and privacy practices.

Where a consent message is required, such as for visitors in the EEA, United Kingdom or Switzerland, Elementle’s Google tag starts with ad and analytics storage denied until a consent management platform sends a choice. Before serving personalised ads in those regions, the site operator must enable and configure a Google-certified CMP in AdSense Privacy & messaging. Once that message is active, use the Privacy choices link in the footer to revisit the available choices. This notice does not replace the consent message supplied by that CMP.
