About Elementle

Elementle is a free daily guessing game that turns the periodic table into a small, repeatable science puzzle.

Why a game about elements?

The periodic table can feel like a wall of names and symbols when it is approached as a list to memorise. Elementle takes a different route. Each puzzle asks players to make a hypothesis, test it against a clue and refine the next guess. Atomic number, family and chemical symbols become useful ideas because they help solve a real problem on the board.

One shared puzzle each day

Every local calendar day brings one mystery element and eight chances to find it. The game keeps the format simple: type an element, read the feedback, and decide what the next clue means. The daily format makes it easy to play for a few minutes, compare approaches with friends and return to the table over time.

Built for curiosity, not just recall

Elementle’s hints connect elements to their properties, discoveries and everyday roles. A clue may lead from a familiar object to an unfamiliar symbol, or from a chemical family to a surprising use.

Independent and educational

Elementle is an independent project for people who enjoy word games, science and learning through play. It is not affiliated with Wordle, its creators or any textbook publisher. The game is designed for general interest and education; its short facts are prompts to explore, not laboratory, health or safety advice.
